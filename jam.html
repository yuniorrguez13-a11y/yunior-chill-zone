Make a game. Any game. It has to fit in one HTML file and it has to be yours. Upload it here before the month runs out, then play everyone else's and vote. At the end, the site's AI plays every entry and picks the winner, and the community picks its own.

Best gameJudged on fun, polish and originality. An exclusive title, name colour and 1,000 denarii.
People's choiceThe entry with the most community votes. An exclusive title and 500 denarii.

The rules

  • One file. A single .html with the CSS and JavaScript inside it, up to 1.5 MB. Images and sounds go inline (data: URLs) or come from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
  • Made by you, this month. Libraries are fine; someone else's game with a new title is not.
  • One entry per person. You can update it as often as you like until the deadline.
  • It runs in a sandbox: no localStorage, no cookies, no popups, no requests to other sites. Keep your save data in memory.
  • Keep it chill. Nothing hateful, nothing stolen. Staff can remove an entry.
  • Your code is public. Anyone can read it. That is part of the fun.

Tip: press "Try it" before you submit. It runs your file in exactly the frame everyone else will see.
