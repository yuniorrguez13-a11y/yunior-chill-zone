1 Identity —

Who is this fighter? The tier badge updates live from your numbers — same formula the game uses.

2 Stats, movement & passives

Since the Freedom Update there are no balance limits — only wide security bounds (shown next to each field). Passives are optional; neutral is regen 0, damage taken 100%, meter 100%, lifesteal 0. The tier badge will tell everyone exactly what you built.

3 Sprites (optional — skip for a puppet body)

Upload the individual frame images of each animation (select several files at once, in order). The creator removes flat backgrounds, trims, aligns feet and builds the sheet automatically. Red-marked animations are required if you use sprites at all. Click a preview to play it.

4 Moves

The 8 core slots: 3 standing attacks, 2 crouching, 1 air, the throw and the super — plus an optional Burst (all three buttons during hitstun, costs half meter) and as many custom moves as you like, reached by chaining: set "Chain on Light/Heavy/Special" and pressing that button near the end of a move flows into the next one. The red box in the preview is where the move hits, drawn to scale. Times are in frames (60 per second).
Fighter at scale, facing right. red = hitbox · cyan = body · amber dot = projectile spawn.
