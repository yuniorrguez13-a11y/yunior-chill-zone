Parasoul runtime sprites

Source: Attack_WebP.zip and Others_WebP.zip supplied by the repository owner.
The original 1,484 individual WebP frames were sampled, scaled to 32%, aligned
to per-animation cells, and recomposed as horizontal WebP strips for SFFG.
