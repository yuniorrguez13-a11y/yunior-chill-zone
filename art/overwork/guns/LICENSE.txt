Weapon models used by Pitty Striker (overwork.html / ow-striker.js).

Source: the "Ultimate Guns Pack" (glb), supplied by the site owner in
September 2026. Five models were kept out of the pack and renamed for the
game:

  knife.glb  <- Bayonet.glb
  glock.glb  <- Pistol-J3i9KDQ3kt.glb
  ar.glb     <- Assault Rifle-Bgvuu4CUMV.glb
  ak.glb     <- Assault Rifle.glb
  awp.glb    <- Sniper Rifle-TKaBjAEofL.glb

Nothing else was changed: no geometry was edited and no texture was added.
The game recolours the models' existing named materials (Wood, Metal,
DarkMetal, LightMetal, Black, Grey, Green, Main...) to show skins, and never
paints a brand name or logo onto them.

The pack shipped without a licence file. The owner supplied it and, on
7 September 2026, confirmed that the material he provides is free of
copyright — that is why none of the packs he sends carry licence text.

Provenance is written down here so every file in the repository can be
traced back to where it came from. If that ever needs revisiting:
these five files come out and the code falls back to the primitive
gun models it still carries and still builds.
