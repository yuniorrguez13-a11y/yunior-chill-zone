Character models used by Pitty Striker (ow-striker.js).

  soldier-owner.glb   the figure you see: helmet, goggles, balaclava, plate
                      carrier, knee pads, backpack. 7,104 triangles, one mesh,
                      one texture. Supplied by the site owner on 7 Sep 2026.

  soldier.glb         <- Adventurer.glb, from the City Pack. Present for its
                      SKELETON and its twenty-four animation clips, not for its
                      appearance, which is never drawn.

Why there are two. The owner's soldier arrived as a single static mesh with no
skeleton and no animations, so on its own it can only slide around the map like
a statue. At load time the game rigs it: `autoSkin` in ow-striker.js weights
every vertex to the nearest bone segments of the other model's skeleton, and the
result is a SkinnedMesh that plays that model's clips (Idle_Gun, Run_Shoot,
Death and the rest) wearing the owner's geometry. The donor's own meshes are
hidden.

Nothing was edited in either file. No geometry was changed, no texture was
repainted, and no brand name or logo appears on either.

Both shipped without a licence file. The owner supplied them and, on
7 September 2026, confirmed that the material he provides is free of
copyright — that is why none of the packs he sends carry licence text.

Provenance is written down here so every file in the repository can be
traced back to where it came from. If that ever needs revisiting: drop
soldier-owner.glb and the bots wear the donor model, which is rigged and
complete on its own; drop both and they fall back to the capsule figure the
game still carries and still builds underneath.
