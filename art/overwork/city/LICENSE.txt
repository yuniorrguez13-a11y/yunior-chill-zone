City models used by Pitty Striker (ow-striker.js).

Source: the "City Pack" (glb), supplied by the site owner in September 2026.

Nothing else was changed: no geometry was edited and no texture was added or
repainted. The game scales each model to a target size at load time (the pack's
scales are inconsistent — a building arrives 3.5 units tall and a hydrant 232),
tints the existing materials for the time of day, and merges them by material.
No brand name or logo is painted onto anything.

The rest of the pack was left out. That includes every character model and the
bicycle, and it includes the two cars from the separate "skibidi" upload: the
Lamborghini carries the manufacturer's badge on its nose, which is a trademark,
so it is not in the repository.

The pack shipped without a licence file. The owner supplied it and has been
asked to confirm that its licence allows use in this game; if it does not, this
whole folder comes out and the arena loses its skyline and nothing else — the
city is decoration outside the walls, it is not part of the map.
