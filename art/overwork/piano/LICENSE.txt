Piano samples in this folder come from the tonejs-instruments sample
pack by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky (https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments),
"samples/piano". The pack licenses its samples under Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Eighteen notes were kept (A1, C2, D#2, F#2, A2, C3, D#3, F#3, A3, C4,
D#4, F#4, A4, C5, D#5, F#5, A5, C6), trimmed to 6.6 seconds with a fade
and re-encoded as 32 kHz mono MP3 at 64 kbps so they load fast. Nothing
else was changed: what you hear in Overwork is a recorded piano.
