Wearable models used by Overwork (overwork.html).

This file covers only the four .glb files sitting directly in this folder:

  cap.glb
  frog.glb
  glasses.glb
  headphones.glb

They are the hats and accessories the courier can put on in the locker. Each
subfolder here carries its own LICENSE.txt for what it contains:

  chars/     the soldier the Pitty Striker bots wear
  city/      the buildings and street props around the Pitty Striker arena
  guns/      the five weapon models
  piano/     the sampled grand piano (CC BY 3.0 — see that file, it is the one
             asset here that came with real licence terms and they are honoured)
  sfx-guns/  the gun recordings

Source: supplied by the site owner in September 2026. Nothing was changed: no
geometry was edited and no texture was added. The game bakes each model to
world space once, centres it per mesh and fits it to the head with the measured
offsets in ACC_FIT. No brand name or logo is painted onto any of them.

The models shipped without a licence file. The owner supplied them and, on
7 September 2026, confirmed that the material he provides is free of
copyright — that is why none of the packs he sends carry licence text.

Provenance is written down here so every file in the repository can be
traced back to where it came from. If that ever needs revisiting: these four
files come out, the locker loses the hat options, and the courier keeps his
built-in cap and hair, which are original geometry.
