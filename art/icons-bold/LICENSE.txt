Bold icon set, used across the site through ycz-icons.js.

Thirty-six white PNGs, trimmed to 160px squares and rendered as CSS masks so
they take currentColor. Names are whitelisted in YCZ_BOLD; reach them with
icon('b:gift') or <i data-ic="b:gift">.

Source: Itcherpro's free game-asset pack, supplied by the site owner in
September 2026. The only change is the trim and the resize; no glyph was
redrawn and nothing was added.

The pack's brand logos (Steam, Twitch, YouTube, Apple, Facebook) were
deliberately left out. Those are trademarks and they do not belong in this
interface whatever the pack's terms say.

The zip carried no licence text. The owner supplied it and, on 7 September
2026, confirmed that the material he provides is free of copyright — that is
why none of the packs he sends carry licence text.

Provenance is written down here so every file in the repository can be traced
back to where it came from. If that ever needs revisiting: this folder comes
out and every b: icon falls back to the stroke set, which is original inline
SVG and covers the interface on its own.
