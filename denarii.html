Denarii

Yuniors Chill Zone · site currency · not real money

Denarii are the Chill Zone's way of saying thanks for being around. You earn them by doing the things you were already doing — chatting, playing, making fighters — and you spend them on cosmetic bragging rights. Nothing here costs real money, and nothing here can be turned into real money. It's reputation with a number on it.

Say it right

1 denarius · 2 denarii · 0 denarii

The name is Latin — a denarius was the everyday silver coin of the Roman Empire. Latin makes its plurals its own way, so one coin is a denarius and any other amount is denarii. The site will remind you of this under your balance, every single time: "One denarius, two denarii. It's Latin. Yes, really."

Why give a site full of stupidly funny games an imperial currency? Because the mismatch is the joke. Same reason the fighting game is "for Dummies".

Where it lives

Your balance is the gold coin at the top of every page — the chat, SFFG, VideoZone and Qmages all show it. Every time the server hands you denarii the coin bumps, a little +1 floats off it and a note in the corner tells you why. That happens live, whatever page you're on: someone playing your fighter while you chat still shows up.

Tap the coin (or the coin in the left rail of the chat, or type /denarii in any channel) to open the Treasury: your balance, your day streak, today's earnings against each cap, your recent activity, the shop and the leaderboard.

Streaks

Post at least once a day and your day streak goes up by one. Miss a whole UTC day and it drops back to zero. Every 7th day in a row (7, 14, 21…) pays a 50 denarii bonus on top of the usual daily 10. The flame in the Treasury shows where you are.

Caps exist for one reason: so a script that posts every ten seconds all night earns exactly the same as a person who said hello. Farming with a second account is pointless too — denarii can't be sent anywhere, so an alt's balance just sits there.

How you spend

The shop sells two kinds of cosmetics. Titles are a short amber label next to your name in chat, in the member list and on your profile card. Name colors paint your name itself — everywhere it appears. You can wear one title and one color at a time, swap whenever you like, take them off whenever you like, and you keep everything you've bought forever.

Staff keep their role colors: an admin's name stays cyan and the owner's stays red no matter what color they bought. Everyone else's name shows the color they equipped.

The leaderboard

The Treasury's third tab ranks everyone by denarii earned all-time — spending doesn't move you down. Top three get gold numbers; your own row is highlighted, and your rank is shown underneath even if you're not in the top 20. Tap a row to see that person's profile.

Troubleshooting

I sent a message and the coin didn't move

Normally the coin bumps within a second. If it didn't, check the Treasury's "Earned today" list: the daily bonus is once per UTC day, and messages stop paying after 20 in a day. A row marked max is done for today. If the coin shows … or nothing, reload the page.

My streak reset

Streaks count UTC days. If your last message was at 7 PM Eastern on Monday and your next one at 9 PM Eastern on Tuesday, that's Tuesday and Thursday in UTC — Wednesday was missed. Post earlier in your day and it's never a problem. A streak that's still alive but hasn't been extended today shows yesterday's count until you post.

I bought a color and my name didn't change

Buying and equipping are two steps. Open the shop, find the color marked Owned and press Equip. Admins and the owner keep their role color on purpose.

"Not enough denarii — you need N more"

Exactly what it says. The number is how far you are from that title. Come back tomorrow, or pick a cheaper one — the price is shown next to every title you don't own yet.

"You already own that"

You bought it earlier. It's in the shop with an Owned tag and an Equip button — you don't pay twice.

My title doesn't show on my older messages

By design. A message wears the title you had when you posted it. Change titles and only new messages pick up the new one. Think of it as a timestamp with attitude.

I hit a daily cap

The "Earned today" list marks it as daily cap reached. That reason is done for the day; the other reasons still pay. Everything resets at midnight UTC.

I changed my username — did I lose anything?

No. Denarii and titles belong to your account, not your name. Rename all you like.

Can I gift or trade denarii?

No, and that's on purpose. The moment coins can move between accounts, people make fake accounts to farm them. Keeping every wallet sealed is what keeps the whole thing honest.

"Couldn't load your wallet"

Either the currency isn't switched on yet for the site, or your connection hiccupped. Refresh the page and open the Treasury again. If it keeps happening, tell the owner in #global.

Somebody is clearly cheating

Site staff can add or remove denarii from any wallet, including down to zero. Report it in #global and a mod will take a look. Because caps are enforced by the server and nothing transfers, "cheating" mostly amounts to a lot of wasted effort.

FAQ

Is it real money?

No.

Will it ever be?

Not planned. Denarii are a thank-you, not a product.

Can I buy denarii?

No. You can only earn them, and everyone earns them the same way.

What's coming to the shop?

More cosmetics. When the next game lands, weapon skins — looks only, never stats. Nothing you buy with denarii will ever make you hit harder or shoot straighter.

Do I earn denarii from the arcade games or high scores?

No. Those scores live only in your browser, so the server can't verify them — and anything the server can't verify can't pay out. Earning is tied to things the database sees happen.

Why "Denarius"?

Because a goofy site deserves an imperial currency, and because it's funny to hear people argue about the plural.
