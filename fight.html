How to play

Best of 3 rounds, 99 seconds each. Hold away from your opponent to block. Light attacks are fast, heavies hurt, specials have a gimmick per fighter. One air attack per jump.

Online

Use either control set. Esc opens the forfeit prompt. The match runs peer-to-peer — the number in the corner is your connection delay.

Tiers

Every fighter gets a power score computed from its stats and frame data — that's the tier badge on the select screen. When community fighters arrive, the engine rates them the same way so matchmaking can keep things fair.
